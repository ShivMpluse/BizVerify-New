# BizVerify Landing Page

Build a modern, high-converting B2B SaaS landing page for "BizVerify" using React, Tailwind CSS, and Lucide-react icons. 

Brand Identity & Aesthetics:

- Theme: Clean, modern fintech/security vibe (Stripe or Vercel inspired).

- Palette: Trust-focused deep navy/slate (#0F172A), crisp white/light gray background (#F8FAFC), subtle borders (#E2E8F0), and a primary vibrant trust-blue or emerald green (#2563EB or #059669) for verified states and CTAs.

- Typography: Inter or Geist sans-serif, high legibility, professional micro-copy.

Page Layout & Sections:

1. Navigation Bar (Sticky):

   - Logo: "BizVerify" with a subtle shield/badge checkmark icon.

   - Links: "How it Works", "Features", "Use Cases", "FAQ".

   - CTA Buttons: "Login" (ghost) and "Get Verified" (primary filled button).

2. Hero Section:

   - Headline: "Verify Your Domain. Build Digital Trust."

   - Subheadline: "BizVerify helps businesses verify ownership of their website domain and showcase their verified status with a professional digital trust badge."

   - CTAs: "Get Verified" (primary with arrow icon) and "How It Works" (secondary/outline).

   - Visual Element (Right or Floating Below): An interactive preview of the live verification badge and a mini mock browser showing a verified status popover.

3. Value Proposition / Mini-Feature Bar:

   - "Build Trust With a Verified Badge" — simple 3-pill banner: Simple • Transparent • Verifiable. DNS-based verification context.

4. 4-Step Process ("How BizVerify Works"):

   - Visual timeline or 4-column card grid:

     01. Create Your Business Profile (Add name, domain, basic details)

     02. Verify Your Domain (Add unique TXT record to DNS)

     03. Get Your Verified Badge (Instant badge availability upon check)

     04. Let Customers Verify (Embed badge with click-through public verification)

5. Live Badge Preview & Feature Breakdown:

   - Split layout:

     - Left side: 4 key checkmarks with icons: Domain Verified, Business Profile, Active Status, Verification Date.

     - Right side: Realistic UI mock card representing the "Public Verification Profile":

       - Header: ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (with green check badge: Domain Verified)

       - Rows: Website: abccompany.com | Status: Active (glowing green dot) | Verified: September 2026 | ID: BV-982143.

6. Use Cases ("Designed For Modern Businesses"):

   - 5 interactive cards or modern tabbed cards: Startups, E-commerce Businesses, SaaS & IT Companies, Agencies & Service Businesses, B2B Businesses.

7. FAQ Section:

   - Collapsible modern accordion (clean border separators, smooth toggle) containing the provided 7 Q&As (DNS verification, badge locations, government certification disclaimer, meaning of Domain Verified, etc.).

8. Pre-Footer Call to Action (CTA):

   - Headline: "Simple Verification. Visible Trust."

   - Subtext: "Your customers shouldn't have to guess whether they're visiting the right business website."

   - Button: "Get Verified Now".

9. Footer:

   - 4-column minimal footer with tagline "Create. Verify. Build Trust.", navigation links, status indicators, and legal/disclaimer links.

Component Requirements:

- Make all cards hover-reactive with soft shadows and border highlights.

- Fully responsive for mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.

- Clean semantic HTML with accessible contrast.
